About WASSA WASSA

WASSA WASSA is a small 3-room inn in Ogimi Village, northern Okinawa — one of the world's famous Blue Zones, known for its extraordinarily long-lived community. Opened in 2014, the inn is run by husband-and-wife team Yuji and Risa Mizokawa, both trained chefs who left Tokyo to bring their culinary craft to the heart of Yambaru.

Facing Nerome Beach and backed by subtropical jungle, the inn offers an unhurried pace and a deeply personal welcome that larger resorts simply cannot provide.

For Families

Step outside into nature's living classroom. Search for creatures along the shoreline, then gather under a sky full of stars to share the day's adventures. Meals are prepared with care — local seasonal ingredients, allergy-friendly on request. With no TV in the Superior rooms, conversation flows naturally around the dinner table.

For Couples & Groups

Friends, multi-generational families, anniversaries — WASSA WASSA accommodates up to 4 guests per room (or more across rooms), offering a private, unhurried experience far from the tourist crowds of southern Okinawa.

By Car (Recommended)

A rental car is essential — public transport in northern Okinawa is very limited.

  • From Naha Airport: approx. 1 hour 45 min via National Route 58
  • From Kyoda IC (expressway): approx. 45 min via Nago-Higashi Road
  • Landmark: approx. 500m north of Michi-no-Eki Ogimi roadside station, on the right-hand side heading toward Kunigami Village

By Bus

Okinawa Bus Route 67 (Yambaru Express) runs from Naha Bus Terminal to northern villages, stopping near Ogimi. Journey approx. 2.5 hours. A rental car or taxi is still needed for the final stretch.

Nearby Attractions

Explore Yambaru & Northern Okinawa

Right at Your Doorstep

Nerome Beach (根路銘海岸)

The beach directly in front of WASSA WASSA. Excellent for snorkelling — clear water, rich marine life. Suitable for all ages. Jellyfish protection required year-round: long-sleeved rash guard, leggings, gloves, and marine shoes recommended. Rental gear available at the inn.

Activities

Hentona Sabani — Traditional Okinawan outrigger sailing boat tours. A rare, hands-on cultural experience on the East China Sea.

Yambarugreen — Nature experience tours through the UNESCO Yambaru forest. Wildlife spotting, trekking, and forest therapy.

Bunasauna — A low-temperature herbal sauna using local Okinawan herbs. A deeply relaxing wellness experience.

Asmui — A spiritual hike through the sacred grove of Ryukyuan mythology.

Okumanavi — Marine activities and local dining, set in a beautiful northern bay.

Ta-taki Waterfall (ター滝) — A short jungle hike through dense Yambaru forest to a freshwater waterfall. Popular for cooling off in summer.

Toy Forest Museum (おもちゃの森美術館) — Charming museum inside Kunigami Village forest park.

Sealife — Diving and marine activities in Okuma, Kunigami Village.

Culture & Crafts

Kijoka Bashofu Hall (芭蕉布会館) — Watch artisans weave bashofu, a traditional Okinawan textile made from banana plant fibres. A UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage craft, kept alive only here in Ogimi.

Dining Nearby

  • Minato Shokudo (みなと食堂) — Local fish and Yambaru ingredients
  • Okinawa Cacao — Bean-to-bar chocolate using local Okinawan cacao
  • Yambaru Rail (やんばるレイル) — Self-sufficient-style book café
  • Emi no Mise (笑味の店) — Traditional Okinawan home cooking by local grandmothers

Major Attractions (Day Trips)

  • Junglia (new theme park, Kunigami Village)
  • Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium (approx. 40 min)
  • Nakijin Castle Ruins — UNESCO World Heritage Site (approx. 30 min)

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Can I check in early (before 16:00)?

Early check-in is not available. The team is often out for grocery shopping, food preparation, and errands before 16:00. Please plan your arrival for 16:00 or later.

Is late check-out possible?

The car park is available until 12:00 at no charge. Late check-out (room use until 12:00) is available for ¥2,200 per person per hour.

Can you accommodate food allergies?

Yes. Please inform us of any allergies or dietary requirements at least 3 days before arrival and we will do our best to accommodate.

Should I bring pyjamas?

Yes, please bring your own. We do not provide yukata or pyjamas as part of our amenities.

Are there many insects?

Yes — we are surrounded by subtropical nature. This includes fireflies, of which 45 species inhabit the area year-round. Insect repellent is recommended.

Do you have English-speaking staff?

Our English conversation ability is limited, but we put our heart into every meal and every interaction. We will do our best to make you feel at home.

Is there a TV in the room?

Superior Twin rooms do not have a TV (available on request). The Deluxe Twin villa has a TV.

What about bedding for 3+ guests in a Superior Twin?

The room has 2 beds. For 3 or more guests, additional futons are provided — guests are asked to set them up themselves.

How large is the Superior Twin room?

Approximately 20 m².

What should I know about the beach?

Jellyfish are present throughout the year. We strongly recommend a full rash guard, leggings, gloves, and marine shoes when entering the water. Rental gear is available at the inn.

Is the inn suitable for guests with mobility limitations?

The inn is not wheelchair accessible. Please contact us in advance if you have specific requirements.

Important Notes

Please read before your stay

  • Jellyfish protection is required when swimming — rash guard, leggings, gloves, and marine shoes.
  • Rooms are not cleaned during multi-night stays.
  • In strong wind or rain, the terrace may be closed and meals served in-room.
  • Power outages are possible during typhoons.
  • During severe typhoons, meals may not be provided.
  • WASSA WASSA is a small inn run by two people — we cannot provide the level of service of a large resort, but we offer something far more personal.
